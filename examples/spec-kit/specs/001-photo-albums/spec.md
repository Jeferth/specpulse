# Feature Specification: Photo Albums

**Feature Branch**: `001-photo-albums`
**Created**: 2026-10-20
**Status**: Draft

## User Scenarios & Testing *(mandatory)*

### User Story 1 - Organize photos into albums (Priority: P1)

**Acceptance Scenarios**:

1. **Given** a library of photos, **When** I create an album, **Then** it appears on the home page
2. **Given** an album, **When** I drag a photo onto it, **Then** the photo moves into the album

## Requirements *(mandatory)*

- **FR-001**: Users MUST be able to create, rename and delete albums
- **FR-002**: Albums MUST be grouped by date on the home page
